About

Researcher. Builder. Problem solver.

I'm Yash Raj Verma, a PhD researcher in Computer Science at IIT Kanpur, advised by Prof. Subhajit Roy. My research sits at the intersection of Generative AI, LLMs, and real-world applications — from education and software engineering to security.

Before my PhD, I worked as an Automation Engineer at Wipro Technologies and completed a research internship at IBM Research Lab, where I worked on improving code LLMs using program analysis.

I've been selected for the Amazon Machine Learning Summer School (top 1 percentile across India) and have published at ACL and other venues. I also hold a patent filed with DRDO.

Outside research, I build practical AI applications — and I'm now available to deliver custom AI solutions for businesses.
